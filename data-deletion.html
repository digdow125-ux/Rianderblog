Data Deletion Instructions

RIANDERBLOG respects your privacy. You can request deletion of personal information associated with your RIANDERBLOG account or your use of the RIANDERBLOG Promotion Hub.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to digdow125@gmail.com with the subject RIANDERBLOG Data Deletion Request.
  2. In the message, tell us the email address used for your RIANDERBLOG account and whether you want your RIANDERBLOG account, connected Facebook/Instagram information, or both deleted.
  3. We may contact you to verify that you own the account. Do not send us your password or Facebook/Instagram login details.

What we delete

After verifying your request, we will delete or anonymize the applicable account information and data held by RIANDERBLOG, including relevant profile details and stored social-account connection information, where deletion is technically possible and not restricted by law.

Facebook and Instagram connections

You can also remove RIANDERBLOG's access from your Facebook account's Business Integrations or Apps and Websites settings. Removing access stops future access but does not by itself send us a request to delete information already stored by RIANDERBLOG. To request deletion of that information, email us using the steps above.

Processing time

We aim to respond to verified deletion requests within 30 days. If we must retain limited information for security, accounting, or legal reasons, we will retain only what is required and explain this where appropriate.

Contact

For questions about privacy or data deletion, contact digdow125@gmail.com.
