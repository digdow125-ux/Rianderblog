Important information

These policies apply to use of the RIANDERBLOG marketplace in Malawi. They are written to explain how the marketplace works and what customers and suppliers can expect.

Publication note: These are operational draft policies, not a legal opinion. Before relying on them as final legal documents, RIANDERBLOG should have them reviewed against current Malawi consumer protection, electronic transactions, data protection, tax and other applicable laws. Replace any operational details that change before publication.

Effective date: 12 November 2026 · Last reviewed: 12 November 2026

1. Terms and Conditions

1.1 About the marketplace

RIANDERBLOG is an online marketplace connecting customers with independent suppliers. The platform supports product discovery, customer accounts, orders, payment coordination, supplier communication and order updates. Unless a listing clearly states otherwise, the supplier is responsible for the product and fulfilling the order.

1.2 Accounts and eligibility

  • Provide accurate, current registration, contact and delivery details.
  • Keep passwords and account access secure and notify support if you suspect unauthorised access.
  • Do not impersonate another person, create accounts for fraud, or misuse the platform.
  • Users must have legal capacity to enter into purchases and agreements. A parent or guardian should supervise anyone who is not legally able to contract.

1.3 Listings, prices and stock

Suppliers must keep product descriptions, pictures, prices, colours, quantities and stock information accurate. Images should fairly represent the item. Prices and currency are shown on the listing or checkout. Availability can change, and an order may require supplier confirmation before fulfilment. RIANDERBLOG may hide or remove unlawful, misleading, unsafe or prohibited listings.

1.4 Orders and minimum checkout

  • The minimum order/payment threshold for checkout is MK10,000.
  • The amount payable through PayChangu must remain at least MK10,000. Shopping credit cannot reduce the PayChangu payment below this amount.
  • At an order total of exactly MK10,000, no shopping-credit discount can be applied.
  • Review the item, quantity, delivery details and total before submitting an order.
  • An order is treated as paid only after payment confirmation is received and verified.

1.5 Payments

Customer checkout payments are processed through PayChangu using the payment methods presented in the checkout. Do not pay a supplier privately or outside the platform unless RIANDERBLOG expressly provides written instructions for that order. A screenshot or payment message alone is not confirmation of cleared funds. Payment processing may be subject to provider delays, fees or service interruptions; any applicable charge should be shown before payment.

1.6 Invite and Earn

  • Use your personal referral link or code to invite a new customer.
  • The invited person must register through that referral link or code.
  • When that referred customer completes a successfully paid and completed qualifying purchase with a value greater than MK20,000, the referring customer is eligible for MK1,000 shopping credit.
  • The reward is one-time per referred customer, not one reward for every later order.
  • Self-referrals, fabricated identities, duplicate or coordinated fraudulent accounts, and manipulated orders are not eligible.
  • Rewards are marketplace shopping credit, not cash; they cannot be withdrawn or transferred.
  • RIANDERBLOG may review suspicious activity, explain a decision, and reverse a reward shown to have been issued in error or obtained fraudulently, subject to applicable law.

1.7 Shopping credit

Shopping credit may be used as a checkout discount where the checkout offers it. It is not a deposit account, cash balance or withdrawable money. Credit is subject to the displayed balance and checkout rules. For MWK orders, credit is capped so that at least MK10,000 remains payable through PayChangu. Credit cannot be used to create a negative order total.

1.8 Delivery and fulfilment

Suppliers are responsible for preparing and dispatching accepted orders and for communicating realistic delivery estimates. Delivery timing depends on stock, destination, transport and other circumstances. Customers must provide accurate delivery details and remain reachable. Any delivery fee and delivery estimate should be disclosed before order confirmation where known.

1.9 Cancellations, returns and disputes

Customers may request cancellation before dispatch. Cancellation is not guaranteed once dispatch has occurred, but statutory rights remain unaffected. Damaged, incorrect, materially misdescribed or undelivered orders may qualify for a remedy under the Returns and Refunds Policy and applicable law. Contact support promptly and retain packaging, order details and relevant photos.

1.10 Communication and prohibited conduct

Use marketplace chat respectfully and only for legitimate marketplace purposes. Threats, harassment, scams, false payment claims, malicious files, unlawful content, attempts to access another user's account, and attempts to bypass platform security or payment procedures are prohibited.

1.11 Platform availability and liability

RIANDERBLOG will take reasonable steps to maintain the platform, but temporary interruptions may occur due to maintenance, connectivity, technical faults or third-party services. Suppliers remain responsible for their listings and fulfilment. Nothing in these terms excludes or limits a right or liability that cannot lawfully be excluded under Malawi law.

1.12 Changes, complaints and governing law

Updated terms will be published on this page with a revised effective date. Material changes will be communicated where reasonably practicable. Complaints may be submitted through the official marketplace support channel. These terms are intended to be governed by the laws of the Republic of Malawi, subject to mandatory consumer protections.

2. Privacy Policy

2.1 Information we may collect

  • Account details such as name, email address, telephone number and profile information.
  • Order information such as products, quantities, delivery address, order status and support history.
  • Supplier application and business details, product listings and fulfilment information.
  • Messages and pictures that users choose to send through marketplace chat.
  • Referral codes, referral activity, shopping-credit records and relevant fraud-prevention information.
  • Technical information such as browser/device details, basic logs and information needed to secure and operate the website.

2.2 How information is used

Information is used to create and secure accounts, display listings, process and track orders, coordinate delivery, support customer-supplier communication, operate referral rewards and shopping credit, prevent fraud, respond to complaints, maintain the service and meet legal obligations.

2.3 Service providers and sharing

Information may be shared only as reasonably necessary with the relevant supplier to fulfil an order, PayChangu to process a payment, hosting/database/storage providers that operate the website, delivery providers where used, and competent authorities where legally required. Payment credentials should be entered only into the authorised payment provider's secure payment interface; RIANDERBLOG does not ask users to send passwords or card PINs through chat.

2.4 Photos, messages and public information

Product listings and supplier information submitted for public display may be visible to website visitors. Profile information may be shown to other users where needed for marketplace functions. Private chat messages and uploaded chat images are intended for the participants and authorised platform administrators, not public display. Do not upload private or sensitive information unless necessary.

2.5 Storage, security and retention

RIANDERBLOG uses technical and organisational safeguards intended to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. Information is retained for as long as reasonably needed for marketplace operations, order records, dispute handling, security and legal requirements, then deleted or anonymised where appropriate and technically practicable.

2.6 User choices and rights

Users may request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information through the official support channel, subject to identity verification, legal retention duties, unresolved transactions and other lawful grounds for retaining records. Users may also choose not to provide optional information, but some marketplace features may then be unavailable.

2.7 Cookies and similar technologies

The website may use browser storage or similar technologies to keep a session, remember a cart, preserve referral information or support essential website functions. Browser settings may allow users to clear or block storage, although some features may stop working.

2.8 Children and policy changes

The marketplace is not designed to collect personal information from children independently of an appropriate parent or guardian. We may update this policy as the service or legal requirements change. The current version and effective date will appear on this page.

3. Returns, Cancellations and Refunds Policy

3.1 Before dispatch

A customer may request cancellation through the order or support channel before the supplier dispatches the goods. RIANDERBLOG will check the order status with the supplier. If cancellation is accepted and payment has been collected, the refund will be processed through the appropriate payment/refund process. A cancellation request does not count as accepted until confirmed.

3.2 After dispatch or delivery

Contact RIANDERBLOG promptly if an item is damaged, defective, incorrect, materially different from its description, incomplete, or not delivered. Do not discard the packaging or damaged item until the complaint has been reviewed, unless it is unsafe to keep it.

3.3 How to report a problem

  • Provide your order number and the name of the product.
  • Explain the issue and when you received the item or noticed the problem.
  • Where safe and practical, provide clear photos or video of the item, packaging and any damage.
  • Keep proof of payment and delivery communications.

3.4 Assessment and remedies

RIANDERBLOG will review the complaint with the supplier and relevant service providers. Depending on the facts and applicable law, a suitable remedy may include replacement, repair, return, partial refund or full refund. A supplier's own policy cannot remove mandatory consumer rights. A return may be refused only where there is a lawful and valid reason, which should be explained to the customer.

3.5 Change-of-mind returns

For a change of mind, contact support before returning an item. Acceptance depends on the product type, its condition, supplier arrangements and applicable law. Products that are used, damaged by the customer, personalised, perishable or otherwise lawfully excluded may not qualify for a change-of-mind return. These exclusions do not remove rights relating to defective, incorrect or misdescribed goods.

3.6 Refund method and timing

Approved refunds will normally be sent back through the original payment route where supported, or another lawful method agreed with the customer. Processing time depends on payment-provider and banking procedures. RIANDERBLOG should confirm the approved amount and method to the customer; no fixed processing deadline is promised until the payment provider's operational timelines are confirmed.

3.7 Delivery charges and shopping credit

Whether delivery charges are refundable depends on the reason for the return and applicable law. If shopping credit was used, the order review will account for both the cash payment and the credit used. Any credit restoration or adjustment will be explained to the customer. Referral rewards are not converted into cash refunds unless required by law.

3.8 Disputes

If the customer disagrees with the outcome, they may ask for a review through RIANDERBLOG support and may pursue any remedies available under Malawi law. Nothing in this policy limits statutory consumer rights.

4. Supplier Agreement

This Supplier Agreement applies to any individual or business approved to sell through RIANDERBLOG. By applying to become a supplier or continuing to list products after approval, the supplier agrees to these conditions.

4.1 Supplier information and verification

Suppliers must provide truthful identity, business, contact and payout details and promptly update changes. RIANDERBLOG may request reasonable documents or information to verify a supplier, investigate complaints or meet legal obligations. Approval is not a guarantee of sales.

4.2 Product listings and compliance

  • List only genuine products that the supplier is legally entitled to sell.
  • Use accurate names, descriptions, specifications, colours, prices, stock quantities and delivery estimates.
  • Upload between one and five relevant product pictures where the listing form requires them; pictures must represent the listed item.
  • Do not list counterfeit, stolen, prohibited, unsafe or unlawful goods.
  • Do not make false claims about origin, brand, warranty, quality or availability.
  • Keep stock and prices updated and promptly correct errors.

4.3 Product review and approval

Submitting a product does not mean it is approved or visible to customers. RIANDERBLOG may review, approve, reject, pause or remove a listing for quality, accuracy, safety, legal or platform-policy reasons. Suppliers must not describe a pending or rejected product as approved by RIANDERBLOG.

4.4 Orders and fulfilment duties

Suppliers must monitor incoming orders, confirm availability, prepare the correct products and quantities, package items appropriately, dispatch within the stated or agreed timeframe and provide accurate fulfilment updates. Suppliers must promptly inform RIANDERBLOG if an order cannot be fulfilled. Suppliers must not mark an order dispatched or completed unless that status is accurate.

4.5 Customer service and disputes

Suppliers must respond to reasonable customer questions, cooperate with investigations, provide evidence when requested and honour valid returns, replacements and refunds under the Returns and Refunds Policy and applicable law. Suppliers must not intimidate customers or move disputes off-platform to avoid marketplace review.

4.6 Payments, fees and supplier payouts

Customer checkout payments are collected through the marketplace's PayChangu checkout. Supplier payouts are handled according to the payout process and methods made available by RIANDERBLOG. Any commission, service fee, payout fee, reserve, tax deduction or other charge must be disclosed to the supplier before it is applied. No undisclosed supplier commission or fee is created by this agreement. Suppliers are responsible for their own tax obligations and accurate payout details.

4.7 Customer data and confidentiality

Suppliers may use customer information only to fulfil the relevant order, arrange delivery and handle legitimate after-sales support. Suppliers must not sell, disclose, copy for unrelated marketing, or otherwise misuse customer information. Suppliers must protect order information and promptly report suspected unauthorised disclosure.

4.8 Platform conduct and prohibited practices

Suppliers must not create fake orders or reviews, manipulate referral rewards, misrepresent stock, divert customers to unauthorised payment channels, upload malicious content, infringe intellectual property, or interfere with marketplace security or operations.

4.9 Suspension and termination

RIANDERBLOG may pause listings, restrict supplier functions or suspend an account where reasonably necessary to investigate suspected fraud, protect customers, address serious complaints, enforce platform rules or comply with law. Where appropriate, the supplier will be told the reason and given an opportunity to respond. Ending access does not remove obligations relating to existing orders, refunds, customer data or unresolved disputes.

4.10 Relationship and legal terms

Suppliers operate independently and are not employees, agents or partners of RIANDERBLOG solely because they use the marketplace. Suppliers may not bind RIANDERBLOG to contracts with third parties. This agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of Malawi, subject to mandatory legal protections. If a provision is unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue to the extent permitted by law.
